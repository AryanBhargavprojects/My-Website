# Design System: Aryan Bhargav Portfolio

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Two Ledgers, One Bet"**

The visual system treats a personal portfolio as an annual filing rather than a promotional deck. A cobalt cover makes one blunt opening claim; mineral paper then carries two evidence ledgers, a current thesis, a public build record, and a direct invitation to talk. The mood is candid, exacting, contemporary, and materially flat.

Density is intentional. Large condensed statements create conviction, while monospaced dates and outcomes make claims feel measured rather than embellished. Rules and tonal fields provide all structure; conventional cards, ornamental illustrations, and decorative effects would weaken the premise.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Saturated cobalt cover against cool mineral paper.
- Condensed grotesk statements paired with monospaced evidence labels.
- Square geometry, strict rules, and no shadows.
- Two-column evidence that becomes one continuous ledger on small screens.
- One restrained filing-cover entrance, followed by fast navigation and state feedback.

## Colors

The palette has one assertive voice and a restrained set of paper, ink, and rule neutrals.

### Primary

- **Cobalt Cover:** The dominant identity field for the opening and thesis surfaces. It should carry the highest-conviction statements.
- **Cobalt Deep:** A darker functional accent for outcomes, links, and the strongest contribution cells.

### Neutral

- **Mineral Paper:** The default evidence surface, chosen to feel physical rather than digitally white.
- **Bright Paper:** A restrained highlight for the current ledger entry.
- **Ledger Ink:** Primary text and the closing contact field.
- **Muted Ink:** Supporting prose, dates, notes, and metadata.
- **Soft Rule:** Repeated row dividers and low-emphasis boundaries.
- **Dark Rule:** Structural axes and stronger container boundaries.
- **Chalk White:** Text on cobalt and dark ink fields.

**The One Voice Rule.** Cobalt is the only chromatic accent. Do not introduce competing semantic colors unless the product gains a real state that requires them.

**The Paper Rule.** Evidence lives on cool mineral surfaces, not pure white cards floating over a generic page background.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Archivo with a sans-serif fallback.

**Body Font:** Archivo with a sans-serif fallback.

**Label/Mono Font:** Fragment Mono with a monospace fallback.

**Character:** Archivo uses variable width to move from direct body copy to compressed, high-pressure statements without changing families. Fragment Mono marks dates, totals, metadata, and operational evidence.

### Hierarchy

- **Display:** Heavy, tightly tracked, and condensed. Reserve it for the opening thesis and operating principle.
- **Headline:** Condensed and compact. Use it for section conclusions and major calls to action.
- **Title:** Strong Archivo at roughly 1.25rem to 2rem. Use it for ledger and component headings.
- **Body:** Regular Archivo with relaxed leading. Keep evidence descriptions near 52 characters wide and longer thesis prose near 65 characters.
- **Label:** Fragment Mono at 0.68rem to 0.78rem, usually uppercase with tabular numerals. Raise utility text to at least 0.75rem on small screens.

**The Compression Rule.** Condensed width is for conviction, not every sentence. Body copy remains at normal width for legibility.

**The Evidence Rule.** Dates, measurements, statuses, and caveats use the mono voice; narrative prose does not.

## Layout

The homepage is two sections: a viewport-height cobalt opening and a mineral-paper evidence section. Both use fluid inline padding from 1.25rem to 4.5rem. Major vertical intervals scale with the viewport rather than following a uniform card gap.

The core ledger is a balanced two-column grid split by a one-pixel vertical axis. Introductory and thesis layouts use asymmetric two-column grids so statements and explanation remain visibly distinct. At 900px these supporting grids stack; at 700px the ledgers become a single sequence and the central axis disappears. At 430px page gutters reduce to 1rem.

The GitHub calendar owns its horizontal overflow. Its year grid keeps a 44rem minimum width inside a full-width scroll container, with an explicit swipe instruction on mobile. The document itself must never scroll horizontally.

**The Two-Ledger Rule.** Parallel evidence belongs side by side when space allows and in semantic reading order when it does not.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is entirely shadowless. Depth comes from large color-field changes, one-pixel rules, denser border weights at section headers, and a single bright-paper highlight for the current entry. Nothing floats.

**The Filed-Flat Rule.** Do not use box shadows, glass effects, blurs, or simulated elevation. Separate information with rules, spacing, and material color.

## Shapes

All surfaces, controls, focus treatments, and contribution containers are square. The signature geometry is linear: horizontal ledger rules, a central vertical axis, and a small rotated square where prior evidence converges into the current bet.

**The Square Rule.** Border radius is zero. Rounded cards and pill labels are outside this visual language.

## Components

### Navigation

- A full-width text navigation sits on the cobalt cover with a translucent chalk-white bottom rule.
- Links are plain, compact, and at least 2.75rem high. Hover underlines are visible; focus uses a three-pixel current-color outline with a five-pixel offset.
- On narrow screens, the redundant in-page Work link is removed while GitHub and Email remain.

### Action Links

- Actions remain typographic rather than becoming filled buttons.
- Use bold Archivo, an underlined text treatment, a directional arrow, and a minimum 2.75rem target height.
- External destinations use an up-right arrow. In-page progress uses a downward arrow.

### Ledger Entries

- Each row pairs a fixed mono date rail with a flexible evidence block.
- A soft one-pixel bottom rule separates entries. Outcomes use cobalt deep and the mono voice.
- Project titles are direct external links with a minimum 2.75rem target height. On precise pointers, the compact up-right arrow stays hidden until hover; keyboard focus reveals it immediately without motion, and touch devices retain an underline instead. Focus always preserves a visible outline.
- The current entry may use bright paper, but it stays flush and square rather than becoming a card.

### Thesis Panel

- A cobalt field interrupts the mineral ledger and combines a compressed proposition with normal-width explanatory copy.
- Use generous fluid padding and no border radius, shadow, or decorative iconography.

### Contribution Calendar

- The calendar is a square, one-pixel dark-rule container with a mineral background and a cobalt intensity ramp. Its graph and total are restricted to 2026 rather than GitHub's rolling last-year range.
- It must remain keyboard focusable and horizontally scrollable on small screens.
- Loading and error states use centered mono copy. The error state always preserves a direct GitHub profile link.

### Motion

- The opening behaves like a filing cover being set: its top and bottom rules write from left to right while the thesis and proof labels settle into place.
- The entrance runs once, completes within 700ms, and uses transform and opacity with confident deceleration.
- Project arrows use an interruptible 180ms transform and 120ms opacity transition only on hover-capable pointers. Reduced-motion users and keyboard users never wait for positional motion.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** pair ambitious statements with nearby evidence or an explicit unknown.
- **Do** use rules and spacing to organize dense information.
- **Do** preserve visible focus, 44px mobile targets, strong contrast, and reduced-motion behavior.
- **Do** let cobalt fields mark the opening claim and the current thesis.
- **Do** keep labels factual, short, and typographically distinct from narrative copy.

### Don't:

- **Don't** turn projects into interchangeable rounded cards or a conventional resume grid.
- **Don't** add gradients, shadows, glassmorphism, glow, or decorative badges.
- **Don't** use cobalt as a casual highlight inside every paragraph; its scarcity gives it authority.
- **Don't** hide uncertainty behind startup language, fabricated traction, or unsupported claims.
- **Don't** allow wide evidence such as the contribution calendar to create document-level overflow.
