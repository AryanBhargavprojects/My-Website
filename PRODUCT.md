# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with no build process.

## Users

People evaluating Aryan Bhargav as a potential collaborator, co-founder, investment, partner, team member, employee, or hire. The homepage should serve builders and investors without making employers and recruiters secondary.

## Product Purpose

Give a visitor a fast, candid understanding of Aryan's commercial history, software experiments, current interests, operating principles, and reasons to start a conversation. Success means a visitor can understand the important facts in seconds, inspect more detail without navigating a conventional portfolio, and contact Aryan directly.

## Positioning

The site presents commercial proof and software learning side by side. It does not disguise experiments as wins or treat shipping volume as traction; it shows what earned money, what earned understanding, and the current bet those experiences produced.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from social profiles, GitHub, referrals, applications, and direct outreach. They may scan quickly on mobile or inspect project and contribution evidence on desktop.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The homepage has one section or at most two sections.
- GitHub activity links to `https://github.com/AryanBhargavprojects` and displays a responsive contribution calendar containing only 2026 activity.
- GitHub authentication tokens must not be exposed in browser code.
- Existing analytics remain active.
- Existing laws and coming-soon pages remain available.
- The primary closing contact action opens WhatsApp; email remains available in the main navigation.
- Project claims are limited to facts supported by the current site, the user's Obsidian notes, or direct user statements.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Aryan Bhargav.
- Voice: direct, candid, ambitious, self-aware, and specific.
- Avoid a generic résumé, inflated founder language, and decorative portfolio conventions.
- Prefer reality over optics.

## Evidence on Hand

- Managed more than $50,000 in advertising for the family business.
- Managed more than $200,000 in freelance advertising campaigns.
- Grew an e-commerce business from zero to $120,000 in revenue in nine months and later exited it.
- Built nine software experiments from January through August 2026; software revenue to date is $0.
- The project sequence is AdgenLab (January), Clawlab and Codebridge (February), Clipzy (March), Kapit API (April), Bud Voice AI (May), Orrin AI (June), Ratel Factory (July), and Dolphin (August).

- Project websites are `adgenlab.com`, `clawlab.io`, `codebridge.hey-321.workers.dev`, `clipzy.tech`, `kapit.dev`, `budcanvas.app`, `tryorrin.tech`, `ratelfactory.dev`, and `usedolphin.tech`.
- Current interests include agent-native systems, APIs, MCP and CLI design, commerce, distribution, cybersecurity, Indian defense and deep tech, strategy, game theory, and quantitative trading.
- No testimonials, employer logos, detailed project case studies, or verified exit value are available and must not be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Evidence over résumé theater.
- Delegate execution, never comprehension.
- Show measurable outcomes and explicit unknowns.
- Treat failed bets as useful only when they sharpen judgment.
- Make the next conversation obvious.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The website must be keyboard navigable, responsive on mobile and desktop, maintain strong contrast, expose visible focus states, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
